Measurement contract
The basket is the sum of one standardized package for each configured grocery item—not an average. Product titles, units, quantities, normalized prices, vendor diversity, marketplace exclusions, and statistical outliers are checked before a price is accepted. Failed current retrievals may carry forward the last validated package price, but those rows are labeled and receive only 35% confidence credit. Electricity uses the official BLS U.S. city-average price-per-kWh series. Gold, silver, electricity, $10, and 10,000 sats are references and never enter grocery-basket math.
